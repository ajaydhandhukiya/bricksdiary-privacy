BricksDiary Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 6, 2026

BricksDiary ("we", "our", or "the App") respects your privacy. This policy explains how information is handled when you use BricksDiary.

1. Information We Handle

BricksDiary may use an email address for login or account identification. The App does not require a password for login.

Information you enter into the App, such as customer details, products, orders, payments, and business records, is primarily stored locally on your device using SQLite.

2. Firebase Analytics

BricksDiary uses Firebase Analytics to understand app usage and improve the user experience. Depending on the app configuration, this service may process usage events, device and app information, and app instance identifiers.

3. Firebase Crashlytics

BricksDiary uses Firebase Crashlytics to diagnose crashes and technical issues. Depending on the configuration, diagnostic data may include crash logs, device and operating system details, app version, and app instance identifiers.

4. How Information Is Used

Information is handled to operate the App, support its features, understand usage, diagnose technical issues, and improve reliability and performance.

5. Storage and Sharing

Business information entered into BricksDiary is primarily stored on your device. This policy does not imply that Firebase analytics or diagnostic data remains on your device. Firebase services may process information on their servers.

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by Google Firebase to provide analytics and crash-reporting services.

6. Data Security and Retention

We take reasonable steps to protect information handled by the App. Local data may remain on your device until you delete it or remove the App or its data. Firebase data is retained according to applicable service settings and Google's policies.

7. Your Choices and Data Deletion

You can manage or delete locally stored data through available app or device features. For requests concerning account-related information or questions about privacy, contact us at the email address below.

8. Children's Privacy

BricksDiary is not specifically directed toward children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, contact us so the matter can be reviewed.

9. Third-Party Services

Google Firebase services are subject to Google's applicable terms and privacy practices.
